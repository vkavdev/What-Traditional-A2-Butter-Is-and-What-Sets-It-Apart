# What-Traditional-A2-Butter-Is-and-What-Sets-It-Apart
 Full of nutrients and easier on the stomach, here's what sets traditional A2 butter apart and why it could change your meals.


Traditional A2 butter comes from milk carrying only the A2 beta-casein protein, and that's what makes it easier to digest than the A1 protein in regular butter. We get ours from small family-run Amish farms in Pennsylvania, where the cows graze on grass and never touch grain. There are essential vitamins in this butter and more omega-3 fatty acids than usual, which is good news for the heart. Going with A2 butter means looking after our family's health while the food still tastes good. There's plenty more to get into.

**What A2 Butter Is and Why It Matters**
A2 butter is a dairy product of its own kind, made from milk that carries only the A2 beta-casein protein, and plenty of us digest that more easily than the A1 protein that turns up in regular butter.
Our A2 milk comes only from small family-run Amish farms in central Pennsylvania, which is how the quality stays where it is. Our butter is 100% grass-fed, so the cows never get grain, and that's part of why the flavor is so rich and why it does what it does for us.
It comes salted or unsalted, and the reviews have been glowing, with the ratings to match.
We stay away from factory farms and GMOs, because what we want are pure, wholesome ingredients that match what we believe about sustainability and doing things honestly.

**What A2 Butter Does for Your Health**
Going with A2 butter gets us a delicious spread, and a whole lot of health benefits alongside it.
There's plenty of the essential vitamins A, D, and E in A2 butter, and those look after our hair, our skin, and our nails. The higher omega-3 fatty acid content is good for the heart and gives our immune system a lift.
We like that A2 butter digests more easily, which puts it within reach of anyone who's lactose intolerant. There are no added chemicals or preservatives in it either, so what we're getting is pure nutrition.
It's versatile in the kitchen too, so it does good things for all sorts of dishes while the flavor stays rich.

**A2 Butter vs. Regular Butter: Key Differences**
While we're on the health benefits of cultured butter, it helps to know how A2 butter measures up against regular butter. A2 butter comes from indigenous cow breeds, and it has its own advantages over the A1 protein you get in the conventional stuff.
Here are some key differences:
Easier Digestion: A2 protein is gentler on the digestive system, making it suitable for those who are lactose intolerant.
Nutritional Density: A2 butter is rich in omega-3 fatty acids and essential vitamins, enhancing overall health.
Chemical-Free: Unlike regular butter, A2 butter is free from hormones and antibiotics, promoting a cleaner diet.
Traditional Practices: A2 butter is made using traditional methods, retaining its natural flavor and nutrients.
Going with A2 butter can mean better health for our families.

That creamy texture does something for everything, from traditional dishes like dal tadka through to festive sweets like peda. A2 butter works in homemade sauces as well, and it adds depth to a recipe. It's right for greasing a pan or for making a soup or stew richer.
With its high omega-3 content and the vitamins A, D, and E in it, we're adding nutrition along with the flavor.

Frequently Asked Questions
How Is A2 Butter Produced Compared to Regular Butter?
We make A2 butter using only A2 milk from indigenous cows, with gentle pasteurization and traditional churning. Regular butter is a different story, since it usually has A1 proteins in it and goes through industrial processing.
Can A2 Butter Be Used in Baking Recipes?
Absolutely, A2 butter works in baking. It brings a rich flavor and keeps things moist. Plenty of us find it makes our treats better while giving us what A2 proteins have to offer. Let's bake something delicious.
What Is the Shelf Life of A2 Butter?
A2 butter usually keeps about three weeks in the fridge. Kept tightly sealed, it stays fresh longer than that. Always check for any off smell or discoloration before using it.
Is A2 Butter Suitable for Vegan Diets?
A2 butter doesn't suit a vegan diet, since it's a dairy product. Look at the plant-based alternatives instead, which fit vegan principles and still bring rich flavors and nutrients to whatever you're making.
